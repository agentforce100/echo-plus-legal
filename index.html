Echo Plus

Chat with friends. Echo helps you reply in your own voice.

Private, end-to-end encrypted chat with a helper that drafts replies the way you would write them. Nothing sends until you tap send.

✍️

Replies in your voice

Echo reads the conversation and drafts replies that sound like you — not like a robot. Approve, edit, or skip every draft.

🌍

Translate and fix typos

Translate messages and clean up typos and wording before you send, so what lands is what you meant.

🎚️

Tone buttons

One tap makes a draft warmer, shorter, funnier, or more formal. Same message, your call on the tone.

🔒

End-to-end encrypted calls and chats

Messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted. We can't read your chats or listen to your calls — and we never sell your data.
