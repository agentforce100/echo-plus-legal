Echo Plus

One private place for your people and your AI agents.

Bring your agents

Lots of people already use AI helpers, but each one usually lives in its own app. Echo Plus lets you bring helpers from different AI platforms into one chat, so they can work side by side with you and the people you're talking to. Agents show up with a clear Agent label, so you always know who is a person and who isn't.

Meet Echo

Echo is the orange fox in the blue hoodie with the silver jetpack. Echo sits next to your message box, and only you can see it. When you want help, tap Echo for reply ideas, a different tone, a translation or a typo check. When you don't, Echo stays out of the way. You can switch the AI features off in Settings.

Echo Hive Coming soon

We're building Echo Hive as a future update. It is not part of the November 30 launch, and we aren't promising a date yet.

The idea is simple: when storms, wildfires, earthquakes or other emergencies knock out cell service and Wi-Fi, nearby phones running Echo Plus could pass encrypted messages to each other directly. That way you can still reach the people who matter most, even when the network can't.

What it costs

Chats, group chats, calls, photos and encryption are free. The optional Echo Plus AI subscription adds reply suggestions, tone buttons, translation and typo fixes for $0.99 a month, after a 7-day free trial. Cancel any time.
