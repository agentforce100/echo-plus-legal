# Echo Plus — first two weeks of posts

One post a day for 14 days. Written short enough to work as-is on X, Threads, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn (adjust hashtags per platform: 2–3 max on X/Threads, up to 5 on Instagram). Every claim follows the kit's honesty rules — see `bios.md`.

Site link for "join the beta" posts: https://agentforce100.github.io/echo-plus-legal/

## Day 1 — Hello
Hi, we're Echo Plus 🦊 A chat app for friends: end-to-end encrypted chats and calls, plus a helper that drafts replies in your own voice. We're in beta — and we're looking for testers. Join us 👇

## Day 2 — The blank box
You know the moment. Someone texts you something big and your reply box just… sits there, blank. Echo drafts replies the way you'd write them — you tap, approve, send. Never stare at a blank reply again.

## Day 3 — Your voice, not a robot's
Echo learns your style from the texts you send — your words, your emoji, your "lol" placement. Drafts sound like you on a good day, not like a press release.

## Day 4 — Tone buttons
Same news, three ways to say it. Echo's tone buttons re-draft your reply — warm, funny, short and sweet — until it sounds right. You're always the one who hits send.

## Day 5 — Encrypted, actually
Echo Plus chats and calls are end-to-end encrypted, and your chat history lives on your phone. Private should mean private. 🔒

## Day 6 — Translate
Your friend texts in Spanish. Your Spanish is… Duolingo-day-three. Echo translates both ways, right in the chat — and drafts your reply back in their language.

## Day 7 — Group call, finally
Group calls are here: get everybody on at once, and keep the chat going while you talk. Beta testers, go break it (gently). 📞

## Day 8 — Meet Echo
The fox in the hoodie is Echo. He rides along in your chats, suggests replies when you ask, and remembers the stuff that matters — birthdays, plans, running jokes. Say hi. 🦊

## Day 9 — Honest when it can't
Our favorite feature is an apology. When Echo has no good draft for a message, it says "Echo can't help with this one" instead of making something up. AI should know when to sit one out.

## Day 10 — How the drafts work
Straight up: when you tap Echo, that chat's recent messages and your style go to Google Gemini to write suggestions. That's the only time messages leave your phone unencrypted — and Echo never sends anything for you. You approve every reply.

## Day 11 — Fix typos
"See you at the restaraunt" — we've all sent it. Echo's Fix typos cleans up your draft before it goes out, so your texts look like you meant them.

## Day 12 — Beta invite, Android
Android folks: Echo Plus is in closed testing and we need 12+ steady testers. Encrypted chats, group calls, replies in your voice. Join the test 👇 https://agentforce100.github.io/echo-plus-legal/

## Day 13 — Beta invite, iPhone
iPhone folks: the Echo Plus TestFlight is warming up. Want first dibs on encrypted chat that helps you reply in your own voice? Join the test 👇 https://agentforce100.github.io/echo-plus-legal/

## Day 14 — Thank you + what's next
Two weeks in: thank you to every tester who's broken something and told us. Next up — more helpers, smarter drafts, same rule as always: nothing sends without your tap. Stick around. 🦊

# Video scripts (shorts: TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts)

Format notes: vertical 9:16, 15–30 seconds, filmed as screen recordings of the real app once accounts are live. Captions burned in — most people watch muted.

## Script 1 — "The Blank Box"
- Hook (0–3s): A text lands: "so… did you get the job??" The reply box sits empty. On-screen text: "when you don't know what to say 😶"
- Beat (3–10s): Tap the Echo button. Three draft replies appear, in different tones.
- Payoff (10–15s): Tap one, approve, send. Friend replies "😂😂😂". On-screen text: "Never stare at a blank reply again."
- Caption: Echo drafts replies in your voice. You approve every send. Join the beta 👇

## Script 2 — "Say It Back in Spanish"
- Hook (0–3s): Incoming text in Spanish. On-screen text: "my Spanish is not ready for this"
- Beat (3–9s): Tap Translate — the message flips to English. Echo drafts a reply, translated back to Spanish.
- Payoff (9–14s): Send. Friend: "¡tu español mejoró muchísimo!" On-screen text: "Echo translates. You just chat."
- Caption: Translate both ways, right in the chat. Echo Plus is in beta now.

## Script 3 — "The Typo Save"
- Hook (0–3s): A draft full of typos, thumb hovering over Send. On-screen text: "do NOT send that"
- Beat (3–8s): Tap Fix typos. The draft cleans up instantly.
- Payoff (8–13s): Send the clean version. On-screen text: "Fix typos. Look like you meant it."
- Caption: Echo fixes typos and tones up your drafts — you still hit send.

## Script 4 — "Everyone On?"
- Hook (0–3s): Group chat blowing up: "call?? everyone on?" On-screen text: "the group chat demands a call"
- Beat (3–10s): One tap — group call starts, faces pop in, and the chat keeps scrolling underneath.
- Payoff (10–15s): Laughter, someone types "this is chaos" mid-call. On-screen text: "Group calls + chat at the same time."
- Caption: Group calls are in the beta. Come break them with us 🦊
