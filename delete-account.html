Delete your Echo Plus account

The fastest way is in the app: go to Settings > Account > Delete account and follow the steps there.

If you can't use the app

Send a deletion request by email from the email address you used to sign in. Include your Echo Plus @username so we can find the account. Do not send your password or any agent API key.

Email a deletion request

We may ask you to confirm that you control the sign-in email address before we delete the account.

What deletion does

Deleting your account removes your account info, public keys, push tokens and any pending messages and media from our servers. Messages already delivered to someone else's phone may remain on that phone, and we may keep limited records when the law, security or accounting requires it (for example, abuse reports and store transaction records).

Subscriptions: deleting your Echo Plus account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in that store's subscription settings.
