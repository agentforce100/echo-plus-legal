Echo Plus Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Echo Plus is a private chat app. This policy explains in plain English what we collect, why, and what we never do. "We" means Kingman Legacy Holdings CA01 LLC, doing business as Kingman Digital Labs, the company that runs Echo Plus.

The short version

  • Your messages are end-to-end encrypted. We can't read them.
  • We keep only what we need to run your account.
  • No ads. We never sell your data.
  • The AI helper only sends Google Gemini the messages you allow.
  • You can delete your account at any time.

What we store on our servers

  • Account info: your username, display name, profile photo and the public encryption keys your phone creates.
  • Encrypted messages in transit: our servers pass along encrypted messages until your contact's phone picks them up. We can't decrypt them.
  • Photos and other media: stored encrypted and only temporarily, then deleted automatically.
  • Push tokens: a device code that lets us send you notifications. Notifications contain no message content and no sender name; your phone fills those in after decrypting.
  • Safety records: blocks and abuse reports you make, plus basic technical logs (like the time of a request) needed to keep the service working and secure.

We use Supabase to host our database and servers.

What stays on your phone

Your message history, contact nicknames and your helper's "About" memories stay on your device. They are not uploaded to us.

The AI helper (Google Gemini)

Echo Plus helpers suggest replies using Google Gemini. When you use a helper, we send Gemini only:

  • What you type or ask the helper, and
  • Up to the number of recent chat messages you choose with the messages-back setting (0 to 40). At 0, no chat messages are sent.

Each person controls their own messages. If the person you're chatting with turns their setting down or sets it to 0, their messages are never sent, even if your setting is higher.

We use Gemini's paid API. Under Google's terms, Google does not use this data to train its models and keeps it for about 55 days to check for abuse. Echo Plus does not store your helper requests. You don't have to use the helper at all.

Calls

Voice and video calls are encrypted and connected through Cloudflare's network. We don't record calls.

What we never do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Law enforcement

Because messages are end-to-end encrypted, we can't read or hand over message content. If we get valid legal process (such as a subpoena or court order), the most we can provide is limited account information, such as a username, when the account was created and when it was last active. We review every request and push back on requests that aren't valid.

Safety and abuse

You can block anyone and report abuse in the app. When you report a conversation, you choose what to send us. We may suspend accounts that break our Terms of Use.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in Settings. This removes your account info, keys, push tokens and any pending messages and media from our servers. Data on your phone is erased when you delete the app. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

Echo Plus is for people 17 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 17. If you think a child under 17 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete your information. Contact us to make a request. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app for important changes.
