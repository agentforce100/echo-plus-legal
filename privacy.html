Echo Plus Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Echo Plus is a private chat app. This policy explains in plain English what we collect, why, and what we never do. "We" means Kingman Legacy Holdings CA01, LLC, the company that runs Echo Plus.

The short version

  • Your messages are designed to be end-to-end encrypted, so our servers relay ciphertext without the keys needed to decrypt message content.
  • We aim to keep only what we need to run your account, and we say below how long we keep each kind of data.
  • No ads. We never sell your data.
  • The AI helper only sends Google Gemini the messages you allow.
  • You can delete your account at any time.

What we store on our servers

  • Account info: your sign-in email address, username, display name, profile photo and the public encryption keys your phone creates. We use your email to run and secure your account, verify it, send sign-in and password-reset messages, and help you regain access.
  • Encrypted messages in transit: our servers pass along encrypted messages until your contact's phone picks them up. Messages are deleted once delivered, and in any case within 30 days. Our servers do not hold the decryption keys. Account information and technical metadata are not end-to-end encrypted, and encryption does not cover content you choose to send to an AI feature or include in a report.
  • Photos and other media: stored encrypted and deleted automatically after 30 days (daily cleanup).
  • Push tokens: a device code that lets us send you notifications. Notifications contain no message content and no sender name; your phone fills those in after decrypting.
  • Metadata: account identifiers, sender and recipient, timestamps, delivery status, device and push tokens, and IP addresses — the technical information needed to deliver messages and keep the service working.
  • Safety records: blocks and abuse reports you make, plus call and ring logs (identifiers and times only, kept about 1 day) needed to keep the service working and secure.

How long we keep things

  • Undelivered messages and delivery receipts: deleted on delivery or acknowledgement, and in any case within 30 days; a cleanup job runs hourly.
  • Call signals: deleted on acknowledgement, and in any case within 10 minutes.
  • Photos and other media: deleted automatically after 30 days; cleanup runs daily.
  • Echo AI usage counters: kept about 1 day. Push-delivery throttling records are kept about 1 hour.
  • Call and ring logs: identifiers and times only, kept about 1 day.
  • Abuse reports: reports about a user are kept after that user's account is deleted, with the reported account's identifier removed; reports made by a deleted user are deleted with that account, except reports related to child sexual abuse material, which are preserved for at least one year.
  • Deleting your account promptly removes your account's server data, normally within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law (including child-safety reports preserved for at least one year) and the abuse reports and store records described in the deletion section below.

Server and hosting logs, including IP addresses, are kept by our hosting provider for up to 30 days under our configuration. Billing records are held by the Apple App Store and Google Play under their policies. Encrypted backups, if any, are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted. AI requests are not stored by Echo Plus; Google keeps them for a limited time under its terms (for the paid Gemini API, currently up to about 55 days, which Google may change).

Service providers

We use a small set of providers only to run Echo Plus. They process the limited information described here to provide their service to us; this policy does not authorize them to use it for their own advertising.

  • Supabase: hosting, database, authentication and storage — account info, encrypted messages and media in transit, push tokens and safety records.
  • Google Gemini: the AI helper — the content you ask Echo to use, as described in the AI helper section below.
  • Cloudflare: call connection and relay — technical connection information; call content stays encrypted and is not recorded.
  • RevenueCat, the Apple App Store and Google Play: subscriptions — purchase and subscription status and store identifiers. The stores process your payment details; we do not receive your full card number.
  • Expo, Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: notification delivery — device push tokens and delivery metadata.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting for these web pages — standard web request information when you visit them.

What stays on your phone

Your message history, contact nicknames and your helper's "About" memories stay on your device. They are not uploaded to us.

The AI helper (Google Gemini)

Echo Plus helpers suggest replies using Google Gemini. This also covers Translate, Fix typos/Reword, link summaries and voice-note help when you choose those features: when you tap Translate or ask for a rewrite, the text you select is sent for that request. When you use a helper, we send Gemini only:

  • What you type or ask the helper, and
  • Up to the number of recent chat messages you choose with the messages-back setting (0 to 40). At 0, no chat messages are sent.

Each person controls their own messages. If the person you're chatting with turns their setting down or sets it to 0, their messages are never sent, even if your setting is higher.

We use Gemini's paid API. Under Google's terms, Google does not use this data to train its models and keeps it for about 55 days to check for abuse. Echo Plus does not store your helper requests. You don't have to use the helper at all.

Disappearing messages

If a chat's disappearing-messages timer is on, new messages are removed from participating phones after the chosen time (5 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week). A person who receives a message can still copy, save, screenshot or photograph it, or use another device before it disappears, so disappearing messages are not a guarantee that every copy will be erased.

Calls

Voice and video calls are end-to-end encrypted, peer to peer: the audio and video travel directly between the callers' phones, and when a relay is needed the call goes through Cloudflare's network — the relay cannot hear the call. Group calls use the same peer-to-peer encryption between each pair of phones. We don't record calls. Echo Plus is not a replacement for your phone service and does not connect to 911 or other emergency numbers. In an emergency, use your phone's regular dialer or another telephone service.

What we never do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Law enforcement

Because our servers relay message content as ciphertext and do not hold the decryption keys, we do not have readable message content to provide. What we may have is metadata (account identifiers, sender and recipient, timestamps, delivery status, device and push tokens, and IP addresses), abuse reports, and content you chose to send to an AI feature or include in a report. If we get valid legal process (such as a subpoena or court order), we will provide only what we actually have that the process covers. We review every request and push back on requests that aren't valid.

Safety and abuse

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation and for the other conduct banned by our Terms of Use. You can block anyone and report abuse in the app. When you report a conversation, you send us the reported message and context so we can review it; that reported material is no longer only on your phone. We may suspend accounts that break our Terms. We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and to law enforcement, and preserve the related records for at least one year. When you report a message or media item, including an intimate image shared without consent, you send it to us so we can review and act on it.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app at Settings > Account > Delete account. If you cannot use the app, use our web account deletion page to request deletion by email. Deleting your account promptly removes your account info, keys, push tokens and any pending messages and media from our servers, normally within 30 days, except where we must keep records by law. Kept records include: abuse reports about you (kept with your account identifier removed), reports related to child sexual abuse material (preserved for at least one year), and store transaction records, which are held by the Apple App Store and Google Play, not by us. Messages already delivered to someone else's phone may remain on that phone. Deleting your Echo Plus account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel that separately in the App Store or Google Play. Data on your phone is erased when you delete the app.

Age

Echo Plus is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know about and access your information, and to correct or delete it. California residents: under the CCPA/CPRA you may request to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we hold, access it, correct inaccuracies, or delete it, subject to legal exceptions. The categories we hold are described above: account and contact information, identifiers, encrypted user content we cannot read, usage counts, diagnostic data, subscription status and safety records. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes beyond those permitted by California law. You or an authorized agent may contact us at the email or mailing address below. We may verify that you control the account or email address before acting, and we will respond within the time required by law. We won't discriminate against you for making a request.
