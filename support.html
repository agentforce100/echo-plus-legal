Echo Plus Support

Need help? Write to us at echoplus@mail.grokbot.com. Tell us what happened and what phone you are using, and we will get back to you.

Getting started

Sign up with an email address, then confirm it from your inbox. If the confirmation email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then write to the support email above.

Your messages are private

Chats, photos and calls are end-to-end encrypted on your phone. Only you and the people you are chatting with can read them — not even the Echo Plus server can. Your history and encryption keys live only on your phone. If you lose your phone or reinstall the app, your old chats cannot be recovered — by anyone.

Echo drafts never send on their own

Echo, your AI helper, drafts replies in your voice. You always tap Send, Edit or Skip. Nothing ever sends without your approval.

Calls on iPhone

While the app is open, calls ring with a time-sensitive notification. When the app is closed, you get a notification to open the app to answer.

Report, block, and delete your account

To report a message, long-press it and choose Report. To block someone, go to Settings > Blocked people. To delete your account, go to Settings in the app.
