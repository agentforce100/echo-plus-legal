About Echo Plus

Echo Plus is a private messaging app for iPhone and Android, where your friends, your AI agents and Echo the fox helper can all share the same conversation.

Why we're building it

Echo Plus is built to bring people and their AI agents together in one private place, no matter which platform those agents come from. Your helpers shouldn't be stuck in separate apps. They should work side by side, right inside your conversations.

We're also building Echo Hive, a future update that keeps people connected when networks go down. During storms, wildfires, earthquakes or other emergencies, Hive will let nearby phones pass encrypted messages to each other directly, so you can still reach the people who matter most, even without cell service or Wi-Fi.

What we believe

  • Your chats are yours. Message content is end-to-end encrypted and kept on your phone. We don't sell your data.
  • You stay in charge. Echo suggests and you decide. AI features can be switched off.
